The challenge

Good sites do not wait for a full feasibility study.

For property developers, timing can make the difference between securing a promising plot and losing it to another buyer. Yet deciding quickly is difficult when the site's development potential is still unclear.

SiteScope helps bridge that gap. Instead of beginning with a blank page, developers can immediately explore generated building volumes, compare possible directions and make the investment opportunity more tangible at an early stage.

Limitations

What SiteScope can and cannot do

SiteScope makes early spatial ideas visible, but it does not establish whether a development can be approved or is worth investing in. The results are starting points for a professional assessment, not verified development rights.

▧

Massing, not planning approval

SiteScope generates building massing alternatives. Planning rules and site specific requirements cannot always be translated directly into a reliable 3D model. A qualified person must assess whether each option can actually fit the site and comply with applicable rules.

⌕

No automatic site discovery

The current application does not find available land, monitor listings or identify properties for sale. You choose an existing plot and enter its address. The tool currently supports addresses in Delft only.

▤

No financial feasibility decisions

SiteScope does not calculate investment returns, determine land value or decide if a project is financially viable. It cannot make planning, design or investment decisions on your behalf.

✎
Why you can edit every concept

Because regulatory and practical constraints require human interpretation, SiteScope lets you select and modify the generated masses yourself. Use the alternatives, sunlight studies and renders to inspire a direction, then apply professional judgment and further research before drawing conclusions.

Current scope: Conceptual building massing, manual geometry adjustments, sunlight studies and rendered views for selected plots in Delft, the Netherlands.
